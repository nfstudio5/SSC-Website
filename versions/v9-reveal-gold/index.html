Present

Shira Sue Carmi is a close advisor to founders and CEOs — most of them women — navigating growth, uncertainty and change. She has built and run the businesses herself. The advice begins with the numbers. It does not end there.

The work pairs financial analysis, strategy and a bias for results with what is usually left out of the room: the whole person. Instinct, feeling and the body’s own reactions are treated as data, too.

The business has to work for the CEO in the context of her life.
